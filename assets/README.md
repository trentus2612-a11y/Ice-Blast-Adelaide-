Both halves of a slider must be the same size and shot from the same angle, or
the drag handle will not line up. If you rename a file, change the
`data-before` and `data-after` attributes on the matching `<figure class="ba">`
in `index.html`.

## The four result photos are placeholders

They do not show work this business has done. The two underbody shots are the
same frame with grime added to one, and the engine bay pair looks the same way.

The site presents them under the line "Same vehicle, same day, nothing but
frozen CO2 in between", so they have to be replaced with real before and after
shots of a real job before the site goes public. Same filenames and sizes and
nothing else needs changing.

`docs/startup-plan.html` covers how to shoot them: phone on a cheap tripod,
same spot, same light, every job.
