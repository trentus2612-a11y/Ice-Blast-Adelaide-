How it works

Frozen CO2, not sand

Compressed air fires frozen CO2 pellets at the surface. They hit, the cold shocks the grime loose, and the pellet turns straight to gas. The dirt drops off and the ice disappears.

Nothing abrasive

The pellet is softer than your steel and your paint. It lifts contamination without cutting into what is underneath.

No water

Nothing to dry out, nothing trapped in a cavity rusting later. Safe around wiring, looms and connectors.

No mess left behind

No sand, soda or grit to clean up. The only thing on the floor is the dirt that came off your vehicle.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Will it damage my paint?

No. The pellets are softer than your paint and your steel, so there is nothing there to abrade the surface. It is the same reason it is used on restoration work where the original finish has to survive.

Is it safe around wiring and electrics?

Yes. It is dry and non-conductive, so there is no water getting into looms, plugs or sensors. That is the main reason people choose it over pressure washing an engine bay.

Do I bring it to you, or do you come to me?

Whichever suits you. The workshop is in Golden Grove and that is the easiest option for most jobs. If the vehicle cannot be moved or you would rather it happened on site, we can come to you for a small travel charge. Smaller parts can just be dropped off.

Will it take rust off?

It removes surface grime, grease and loose scale, but it will not cut back heavy rust the way abrasive blasting does. If that is what your job needs we will tell you and point you at the right option.

How long does it take?

An engine bay is usually a morning. A full 4x4 underbody is most of a day. Condition changes it more than size does, which is why we like to see it before locking in a price.

Do I pay a deposit?

For jobs over $1,500 we take a 20% deposit at booking. Everything else is paid on completion once you are happy with it.
