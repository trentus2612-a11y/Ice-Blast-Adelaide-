Three of us, one blasting rig, and a corner of Adelaide nobody is servicing yet.

A working pitch for a mobile dry ice cleaning business out of Golden Grove. Numbers are ballpark until we get real quotes.

What we'd actually be doing

Dry ice blasting fires frozen CO2 pellets at a surface. They hit, freeze the grime, then vanish into gas. No sand, no soda, no water, nothing left to sweep up. It strips grease and filth off an engine bay or a chassis without touching the paint, wiring or rubber underneath.

Everything runs off the ute. We drive to the customer, blast, and leave. No shed to lease, no booth to build, no extraction system — which is exactly why this is cheap to start compared to sandblasting or spray painting.

Who pays us

  • 4x4 and off-road owners wanting the underbody and chassis cleaned after trips. This is our warmest lead — we already know these people.
  • Detailing studios who want to offer it without buying the gear. We become their subcontractor.
  • Restoration and classic car owners. Engine bays and chassis where nothing abrasive is allowed near the original finish.
  • Mechanics and small workshops needing parts cleaned properly.
  • Walk-up engine bay cleans. Cheap, quick, and the job that gets us reviews fastest.

Why Golden Grove

Most of the dry ice operators in SA sit city-side, south or west. The northeast corridor looks wide open. We'd be the local option for Golden Grove, Modbury, Salisbury, Tea Tree Gully and everything out to the hills — and close enough to the city to chase work there too.

Getting it lower again

  • Rent-to-buy the machine. Turns the biggest line on the table into a monthly payment the jobs can cover, and if the work isn't there we hand it back instead of wearing a $6,000 paperweight.
  • Hire a compressor for the first few jobs. Day hire is cheap and we only buy once we know how often we're using it.
  • Buy ice per job at the start rather than stocking up. No point paying for pellets that sublimate in the bin.
  • Don't wrap the vehicle until the calendar is full. Magnets look fine and cost a tenth as much.

On the shoestring column that's about three grand each to have a real business running. With rent-to-buy on the machine it drops closer to fifteen hundred each plus a monthly payment. That's the argument for doing this now instead of talking about it for another year.

  • Work out whose vehicle is carrying the rig
