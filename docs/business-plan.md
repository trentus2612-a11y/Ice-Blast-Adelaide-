# ICE BLAST ADELAIDE
### Dry Ice Cleaning — Cars | 4x4s | Engines | Parts | More
**Based in Golden Grove, SA**

---

## 1. The Idea

Mobile dry ice blasting service covering cars, 4x4s, engine bays and parts cleaning across Adelaide's northeast, based out of Golden Grove. No fixed shopfront needed — the whole business runs off a ute/van with the blasting rig on board, so we go to the customer (or they drop the part to us).

**Why it works:**
- No abrasive media, no secondary waste — pellets sublimate straight to gas on impact
- Non-damaging on paint, wiring, rubber and delicate components — big selling point for 4x4 and classic car owners
- Fast turnaround vs. traditional degreasing/steam/media blasting
- Low barrier to entry compared to sandblasting (no booth, no containment/extraction system required)
- As far as we're aware, minimal direct competition in the northeast/Golden Grove area specifically — most SA operators are city-based or south/west side

**Target customers:**
- 4x4 and off-road owners (underbody/chassis cleaning after trips — natural crossover audience)
- Car detailing studios wanting to offer it as an add-on / outsource it to us
- Classic car and restoration owners (engine bays, chassis)
- Small workshops/mechanics needing parts cleaned
- General public engine bay cleans (entry-level, easy upsell)
